Literature Discovery

Two tools in one tab: (1) search real academic databases for papers, and (2) verify whether your generated text follows proper academic format.

ℹ What this tab does: Database Search — searches OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, EconPapers/RePEc, and ERIC for real published papers, filtered by SJR quartile (Q1/Q2/Q3). Use this to find sources for your research.
Academic Format Verification — paste your generated text (e.g., from Quick Generate) below to check whether it is written in proper academic format: readability, passive voice, academic tone, transition words, and more. You can also copy the text with one click.
